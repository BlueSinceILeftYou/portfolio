Hi, I’m Ethan Cappelleri, and I’m finishing the M.S. portion of Boston University’s combined B.A./M.S. program in Computer Science. I did not take a straight path into CS: I started college expecting to pursue academia and studied philosophy and psychology before finding my way here. What stuck was the ability to learn an idea, build something with it, and test it against a real problem.

That interest has led me to work across machine learning, scientific AI, and reasoning systems. At Argonne National Laboratory, I worked with condensed matter physicists on AI/ML tools for beamline operations; at BU, I study LLM-guided proof finding and teach combinatorics and data structures. Sports analytics is another place I get to apply those interests through research, modeling, dashboards, and computer-vision projects. Outside of work, I enjoy ice skating, chess, watching sports with friends, and spending an unreasonable amount of time managing teams in Out of the Park Baseball.
