“Wanna See Me Do it Again?”: Dynamic Catch Probability Visualization through ManimGL

Summer 2026

with Sam Cowan, Evan Kurtz, and Tejas Rama · 2026 SMT Data Challenge

Honorable Mention

Abstract: We introduce a new type of replay for highlight and lowlight plays in the outfield by integrating a dynamic catch probability model into an animation of real MiLB plays. This work discusses the case for viewing catch probability over both space and time, and provides example animations as they would be seen on an in-stadium scoreboard. These animations seek to improve fan understanding of how each moment between a hit and catch influences the fielder’s probability to make the play. To achieve this, we estimated catch probability at 20 FPS using an XGBoost model, constructed catch probability “clouds”, and implemented 3D player and ball movement by developing a custom, baseball-centric extension to the open-source ManimGL library.

Quantum Kernel Estimation

Nov. 2026

Quantum Machine Learning · Review

Reviews quantum kernel estimation from Havlicek et al., including the classical SVM formulation, quantum feature maps, and circuit-based kernel estimation. Also implements a simulated version using Qiskit on toy datasets and compares quantum kernels with standard classical kernels.

Fine-Grained Complexity of Transformers in the Small Embedding Regime

May 2026

Fine-Grained Complexity · Survey

Surveys conditional lower bounds for transformer self-attention under SETH, focusing on the small-embedding regime. Explains why quadratic attention cost is conditionally optimal and presents an accessible proof of the central reduction.

Save It for Later: Using Backtracking to Value Future ABS Challenge Opportunities

Aug. 2026

Saberseminar 2026 · with Sam Cowan

Presented a backtracking framework for valuing future automated ball-strike (ABS) challenge opportunities. We used league-wide challenge probability models to estimate the opportunity cost of using a challenge, then compared players’ decisions with league average and expressed their impact in runs.
