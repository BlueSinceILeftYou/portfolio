Selected Projects

“Wanna See Me Do it Again?”: Dynamic Catch Probability Visualization through ManimGL

Summer 2026

2026 SMT Data Challenge · Honorable Mention

I developed, trained, and evaluated catch probability models for our team’s animated Minor League Baseball replays, which show a fielder’s chance of making a catch over space and time. I engineered kinematic features and compared logistic regression with XGBoost, evaluating both predictive performance and whether the models captured the nonlinear behavior needed to produce meaningful probability “clouds.” I revised the modeling pipeline to support rapid experiments and prevent data leakage, and corrected a nearest-fielder identification issue that improved our Brier score and resolved inconsistencies in the animations.

Save It for Later: Using Backtracking to Value Future ABS Challenge Opportunities

Aug. 2026

Saberseminar 2026 · with Sam Cowan

We developed a backtracking framework to estimate the opportunity cost of using an automated ball-strike (ABS) challenge. I designed models of league-wide challenge probability and proposed the backtracking algorithm. We then evaluated players’ challenge decisions against league average and estimated their impact in runs.
